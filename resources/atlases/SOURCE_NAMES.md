|---|---|

Only filenames changed; file contents are unchanged. Source links are in `NOTICE_ATLAS_NETWORK_EXAMPLES.md` at the repository root. AAL90 holds the volume, vertex-label table and surface masks used in the tutorials. Schaefer2018/MNI contains 100, 200, 400 and 1000 parcel volumes and their matching seven-network lookup tables.
