# Real-data tutorial and original template resources

The original surface and template resources retain their original coordinates
and contents. The MNI ICBM152 surface and its smoothed derivative were supplied
with permission from Prof. Alan C. Evans and are authorized for redistribution.
The AAL volume, derived vertex labels and bundled AAL/Brodmann Inspect lookup
files are included with their source credits. Attribution to
the atlas authors is retained in the original Reference.txt files and notices.

OneSample_ROI11_NC.nii is supplied and authorized for redistribution by Mingrui
Xia. Source: Xia M, Wang Z, Dai Z, Liang X, Song H, Shu N, Li K, He Y (2014).
Differentially disrupted functional connectivity in posteromedial cortical
subregions in Alzheimer's disease. J Alzheimers Dis 39:527–543.
The demonstration volume is a one-sample PCC-subregion seed connectivity
statistical map in standard MNI space, not a disease-group difference map.

Tutorial02_ICBM152_Vertex_Nodes.node and
Tutorial03_Illustrative_Directed8.edge are project tutorial assets authorized
for redistribution. The directed matrix is illustrative, not empirical.
Original historical example files retain the citations supplied with them;
the newer group network collection has its own atlas network notice.

These resources are visualization examples. Smoothing, interpolation and
cluster-size display filtering do not constitute statistical correction.
No Scene JSON, private processing workflow or excluded template is supplied.
